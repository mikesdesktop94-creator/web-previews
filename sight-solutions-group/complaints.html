Our complaint process

If something has gone wrong — a fee you don't understand, a settlement you didn't authorize, a representative who told you something inconsistent with our disclosures, a communication you asked us to stop — tell us.

  1. Submit it. Email compliance@sightsolutionsgroup.com, call (888) 404-8615, or write to Compliance Department, 2200 Ross Avenue, Suite 1800, Dallas, TX 75201. Include your name, the best way to reach you, and what happened.
  2. Acknowledgement within 2 business days, with a reference number.
  3. Written response within 5 business days — or, if the matter requires investigation, a written status update within 5 business days and a substantive response within 30 days.
  4. Escalation on request. If you're not satisfied, ask for review by the Chief Compliance Officer. That review is completed within 15 business days.

Complaints are logged, reviewed monthly by management, and reported to regulators where required. We do not condition the resolution of a complaint on your agreement to withdraw a regulatory filing, sign a non-disparagement clause, or delete a public review.

If we don't resolve it

You are entitled to complain to a regulator at any time, whether or not you have contacted us first, and whether or not our internal process is finished. Doing so will not affect your program, your fees, or how we treat you.

Your rights as a consumer

  • To cancel. You may cancel your program at any time without penalty and withdraw the funds remaining in your dedicated account. See Section 15 of our disclosures.
  • To pay no advance fee. Under the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule, no debt relief company may charge you a fee before a debt is settled, you agree to the settlement, and a payment is made. If any company asks you for money upfront, report it.
  • To your own funds. Your dedicated account is in your name at a bank unaffiliated with us. You own the money in it.
  • To approve every settlement. No settlement may be accepted on your behalf without your authorization.
  • To be told the truth about results. Guarantees of specific savings or timelines are prohibited. Any company making one is violating federal rules.
  • To stop the calls. Revoke consent at any time; see our Do Not Call Policy.
  • To complain freely. Without retaliation, and without our permission.

Reporting impersonation or fraud

Debt relief is a common target for scams that copy legitimate companies' names and branding. We will never ask you to pay a fee before a settlement, ask for gift cards, wire transfers, or cryptocurrency, ask for your full Social Security number or banking password by email or text, claim affiliation with a government forgiveness program, or pressure you to decide on a single call.

If you receive a communication claiming to be from Sight Solutions Group that does any of these, forward it to security@sightsolutionsgroup.com and report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
