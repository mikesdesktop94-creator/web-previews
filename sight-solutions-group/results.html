What the fee is calculated on

Our fee is a percentage of the balance at the time an account is enrolled, not a percentage of what we save you. Both models are legal and both are used in this industry. Enrolled-balance pricing means your fee is fixed and predictable from day one; it also means the fee does not shrink if a settlement comes in higher than expected. We think that predictability is worth it — but you should know which model you're buying, and you should ask any competitor the same question.

Assumptions in this illustration

Settlements average 51% of the enrolled balance; every enrolled account settles; no additional interest, late fees, or penalties accrue after enrollment; deposits are made on time every month; the dedicated account fee is $10.95/month; and the program runs the full selected term. Real programs violate several of these assumptions regularly. Balances typically grow after enrollment, some accounts never settle, and clients miss deposits. Treat this as a ceiling on the good case, not an expectation.

Methodology

What our published statistics actually mean

“$1.4 billion in enrolled debt resolved”

The sum of the enrolled balances of all accounts that reached a completed settlement between January 1, 2016 and December 31, 2025 for clients of Sight Solutions Group. It counts balances at the time of enrollment, not amounts paid or amounts forgiven. It does not measure client savings, and it does not net out fees.

“Average settlement of 51%”

The arithmetic mean of (settlement amount ÷ enrolled balance) across all accounts settled between January 1, 2023 and December 31, 2025, for clients who remained in a program for at least twelve months. It is calculated before program fees. Including fees at a 22% tier, the comparable figure would be approximately 73% of the enrolled balance. Accounts that never settled are excluded from this average, which biases it favorably — a limitation we are stating rather than hiding.

“Median completed program length: 28 months”

The median number of months between enrollment and final settlement, counting only clients who completed their program. Clients who withdrew early are excluded. Completion rates in this industry are materially below 100%, so this figure describes successful clients, not all clients.

“30 states”

States in which Sight Solutions Group currently accepts new enrollments, holds any license or registration required by that state, and maintains any required surety bond. Availability changes; see state disclosures for the current list.

Limitations that apply to all of the above

  • Past performance does not predict individual results.
  • Averages conceal wide variation. Individual settlements have ranged from roughly 20% to 100% of the enrolled balance, and some accounts do not settle at all.
  • Figures are compiled from our internal systems and, unless expressly stated, have not been audited or verified by an independent third party.
  • Enrolled balances grow after enrollment when interest and fees continue to accrue. Percentages calculated against enrolled balances therefore understate what is actually owed at settlement.
  • Clients who do not complete a program may end in a worse position than when they enrolled.
