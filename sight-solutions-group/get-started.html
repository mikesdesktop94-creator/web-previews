If you are being sued right now

Do not wait on a consultation. Respond to the summons within your state's deadline — ignoring it can result in a default judgment, wage garnishment, or a bank levy. Contact a consumer attorney or your local legal aid office (lsc.gov) immediately. We are not a law firm and cannot represent you.
