Borrow carefully

A personal loan is new debt. Consolidating credit cards only helps if you stop adding to the cards afterward — otherwise you end up owing both. Longer terms lower the payment and raise total interest. If your obligations already exceed what you can repay, a loan may be the wrong tool; nonprofit credit counseling (nfcc.org), a debt resolution program, or a licensed attorney may serve you better. We'll tell you when we think that's the case.

Questions

The things people ask first

How do I find out what I qualify for?

Two ways, and neither costs anything. Call (888) 404-8613 and a licensed specialist will talk through your situation and tell you what's available — no form, no commitment. Or fill out the short form and we'll call you back, usually within one business day, at whatever time you pick.

Is fundii the lender?

No. fundii is a loan comparison and matching service. We are not a bank or a lender and we make no lending decisions. Loans are originated by our participating lenders and their partner banks, each with its own rates, terms, fees, and requirements. Your loan agreement is with that lender, not with us.

What does fundii charge me?

Nothing. No application fee, no service fee, no charge of any kind to you. We are compensated by the lender when a borrower we refer takes out a loan. That compensation can vary by lender, which may influence the order in which offers are presented — so compare the APR, not the position on the page.

Does filling out the form commit me to anything?

No. The form is an inquiry, not an application for credit. It creates no obligation for you and none for any lender. You can stop at any point, and you're free to decline every offer that comes back.

How fast can I get the money?

Most people hear back the same day or the next business day. After you choose a lender and complete its verification — usually identity and income documents — funding typically takes one to three business days, and some lenders fund the next business day. Timing is set by the lender and its bank, not by us.

What is an origination fee?

A one-time fee some lenders charge to process the loan, generally 0% to 8% of the amount borrowed. It's usually deducted from your proceeds — borrow $15,000 with a 5% fee and $14,250 arrives — while you repay the full $15,000. A properly calculated APR already includes it, which is why you should compare APR rather than the interest rate.

Can I pay the loan off early?

Yes. Every lender in our network allows early payoff with no prepayment penalty. Paying ahead reduces the total interest you pay. Origination fees already deducted are not refunded.

Should I consolidate my credit cards with a loan?

Only if two things are true: the loan's APR is meaningfully lower than your cards' blended rate, and you'll genuinely stop charging the cards back up. Consolidation fails most often not because the math was wrong but because the balances came back. If your obligations already exceed what you can repay, look at nonprofit counseling (nfcc.org) or a debt resolution program instead — and ask us, we'll say so.

What information will you ask for?

How much you want, what it's for, when you need it, your income and employment, and how to reach you. That's it to get the conversation started. If you decide to move forward with a lender, that lender will ask for its own documentation and give you its own disclosures before anything is signed.

Are loans available in my state?

Availability, maximum amounts, and rate caps vary by state, and some lenders don't operate everywhere. If no lender in our network serves your state, we'll tell you rather than take your information.

Is my information safe?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we do not sell personal information. We share your details with lenders in our network so they can present offers — that's the service. You can request deletion at any time by emailing privacy@fundii.us.
